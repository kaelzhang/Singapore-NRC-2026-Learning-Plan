# W10 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand food movement as a safety-critical system, not just a delivery game.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Ask what happens if ice cream warms up during transport.
- 25 min: Explain logistics, cold chain, traceability.
- 25 min: Draw a cold food journey.
- 20 min: Robot route and docking challenge.
- 10 min: Failure point discussion.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Logistics is just transport." It also includes storage, records, temperature, timing, and safety.
- "Fast is always best." Safe and accurate delivery matters.
- "Traceability is only paperwork." It is how people act quickly during safety problems.
