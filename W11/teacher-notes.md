# W11 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should distinguish food loss from food waste and understand why prevention is better than disposal.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Food waste quick poll.
- 25 min: Loss versus waste explanation.
- 25 min: Sorting activity.
- 20 min: Robot sorting mission sketch.
- 10 min: Waste hierarchy reflection.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Composting makes wasting food fine." Composting is useful, but edible food should be prevented from becoming waste.
- "Food waste is only a home problem." Waste happens across retail, food service, households, and supply chains.
- "Sorting is easy." Real sorting needs safety, contamination checks, and clear categories.
