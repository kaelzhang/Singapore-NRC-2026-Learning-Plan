# W04 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand why food security is broader than "produce more food."

## Pacing
- 10 min: Review four food-security dimensions from W01.
- 25 min: Read and annotate the SOFI numbers.
- 25 min: Group examples: availability, access, utilization, stability.
- 20 min: Robot contribution sentence.
- 10 min: Exit ticket with one limit of robotics.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Hunger means there is no food anywhere." Often food exists but access, affordability, conflict, or stability fail.
- "Calories are the same as nutrition." Healthy growth needs many nutrients.
- "A robot that increases yield automatically ends hunger." Access and affordability still matter.

## NRC Link
This week supports stronger project explanations. Teams should be able to name which food-security dimension their robot mission supports.

## Evidence Of Learning
Students complete this sentence accurately:

"Our mission idea improves food security because it helps [dimension] by..."
