# W08 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should see agricultural robots as task specialists and learn to break a farm task into sense, move, and act.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Brainstorm hard farm jobs.
- 25 min: Sense-move-act explanation with examples.
- 25 min: Team task decomposition.
- 20 min: Robot sketch and object-handling discussion.
- 10 min: Ethics and worker discussion.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "A robot farmer does everything." Most real systems are specialized.
- "Automation only removes jobs." It can also change jobs, reduce drudgery, and create technical roles.
- "If a robot works in a video, it works everywhere." Farm environments differ by crop, weather, terrain, and cost.
