# W02 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand Singapore's food resilience as a multi-pillar system, not as a simple local-versus-imported debate.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Ask where students think breakfast ingredients came from.
- 20 min: Explain import dependence and the four pillars.
- 30 min: Team poster: Singapore food resilience map.
- 20 min: Robot mission translation.
- 10 min: Exit ticket.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Local is always better." Local production can help resilience, but it must also consider land, energy, labour, cost, and demand.
- "Imports are bad." Imports are essential for Singapore; the issue is diversity and reliability.
- "Alternative protein is automatically allowed." Singapore treats many alternative proteins as novel foods requiring safety review.

## NRC Link
This week supports production infrastructure and logistics thinking. It also helps students explain why a robot task has local Singapore relevance.

## Evidence Of Learning
Students should correctly use at least three of these terms: resilience, diversification, local production, stockpiling, partnership, fibre, protein.
