# W13 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should compare future foods using evidence and tradeoffs instead of novelty or disgust reactions.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Ask what foods students already eat that are plant-based or fermented.
- 25 min: Explain alternative protein categories.
- 25 min: Comparison table.
- 20 min: Safety/logistics robot task.
- 10 min: Fair statement practice.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Future food means lab food only." Many future foods include old ideas such as beans, fermentation, mushrooms, algae, and insects.
- "New means unsafe." New foods need assessment; safety is tested, not assumed.
- "Alternative proteins will replace all farms." A mixed system is more realistic.
