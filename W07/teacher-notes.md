# W07 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand precision agriculture as a decision loop: sense, compare, decide, act, test.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Field variability demo using four paper zones.
- 25 min: Sensors and remote sensing.
- 25 min: If-then decision rules.
- 20 min: Robot route and colour marker activity.
- 10 min: Failure discussion.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "More data is always better." Data must be accurate and useful.
- "Drones solve farming." Drones mostly observe; decisions and actions still matter.
- "AI is always correct." Algorithms must be tested under real conditions.
