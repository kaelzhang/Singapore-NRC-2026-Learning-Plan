# W12 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand climate as a system constraint that affects biology, labour, logistics, and prices.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Ask what heat can do to plants, workers, and food storage.
- 25 min: Explain climate risk, water stress, adaptation, mitigation.
- 25 min: Heat wave chain map.
- 20 min: Resilience robot mission.
- 10 min: Backup plan discussion.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Climate affects only farms." It also affects workers, water, transport, storage, and prices.
- "Adaptation and mitigation are the same." Adaptation reduces harm; mitigation reduces the cause.
- "One technology creates resilience." Resilience usually needs diversity, backup, and planning.

## NRC Link
This week strengthens future-challenge explanations and helps students design missions that respond to shocks, not only normal operations.
