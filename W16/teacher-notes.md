# W16 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should synthesize the whole curriculum into a clear capstone explanation.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Review the 16-week map.
- 20 min: Scenario selection and problem map.
- 25 min: Fill final explanation template.
- 20 min: Add evidence and ethical question.
- 15 min: 90-second presentations.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Big claims sound better." Honest, focused claims are stronger.
- "The robot is separate from the science." The robot models one part of the food system.
- "Future means fantasy." Scenarios should be possible and connected to evidence.
