# W05 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should connect plant biology to robot actions. The key idea is that robots support plant needs by sensing, moving, or controlling conditions.

## Pacing
- 15 min: Plant needs brainstorm.
- 25 min: Photosynthesis and spectrum explanation.
- 20 min: Hydroponics versus soil comparison.
- 20 min: Greenhouse robot checklist.
- 10 min: Vocabulary review.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Plants eat soil." Plants use minerals and water from soil, but they make sugars through photosynthesis.
- "More light is always better." Too much light or heat can stress plants, and artificial light costs energy.
- "Hydroponics is easier than soil." It removes soil problems but adds pump, pH, nutrient, and electricity problems.

## NRC Link
This week supports production infrastructure missions. Encourage students to describe what condition their robot is helping control.
