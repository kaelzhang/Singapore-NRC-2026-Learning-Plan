# W06 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should understand CEA as a controlled system with benefits and costs.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Review plant needs from W05.
- 25 min: Compare outdoor farm, greenhouse, and vertical farm.
- 25 min: Draw and label a vertical farm system.
- 20 min: Robot path design.
- 10 min: Tradeoff discussion.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Vertical farms can grow everything." They are usually better for selected high-value or fast-growing crops, not every staple crop.
- "Indoor farms do not need nature." They still depend on water, nutrients, energy, biology, and human decisions.
- "Automation means no people." Skilled people still design, maintain, clean, monitor, and make decisions.

## NRC Link
This week supports production infrastructure and narrow-space robot design. It also prepares students to think about field layout as a model of a real facility.
