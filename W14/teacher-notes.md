# W14 Teacher Notes

## Aim
Students should translate a mission idea into algorithmic structure with sequence, loops, conditions, variables, and states.

## Pacing
- 10 min: Explain "program as recipe."
- 25 min: Walk through one model mission.
- 25 min: Students write pseudocode for their own mission.
- 20 min: Peer check for missing conditions or unclear order.
- 10 min: Debugging prediction.

## Misconceptions To Watch
- "Blocks make logic automatic." Blocks help expression, but the logic still must be designed.
- "A long program is better." Clear structure is better than length.
- "Debugging means the student failed." Debugging is normal engineering work.
